02
Alarm System
Safe and sound
+

The house is secured by a modern alarm system. The area is known for its general safety — the system is yours to use freely.

False alarms may result in a police response.

The three modes

Unsecured At home, free movement. No alarm.
Secured exterior When everyone leaves the house. Alarm on open doors/windows and motion inside.
Secured interior For the night. Doors/windows alarm only — no motion sensors.

Operation

Turn off alarm Place the key fob in the centre of the panel — wait for the beep. On return: 30 sec to deactivate.
Exterior mode Close everything. Press button (1) and leave the house within 30 sec.
Interior mode Close everything. Press button (2).
